TACTICAL OPERATIONS MANUAL (v0.47.0)

Single 3×3 Start & Line-Drag Barricades

Every map opens with a single 3×3 Safe Zone (+9% DMG). Expand Safe Zones ($10, Key 7) toward breaches; all other buildings & towers build only on Safe Zones. Only Engineers can step over Safe-Zone structures — all other units route around structures and enter/exit hubs from adjacent tiles.

Specialists, Gunner & Hold Stance (4/8/10)

Barracks & Engineering scale 4 / 8 / 10 slots! Train Commandos, Lv2 Gunners ($110), Lv3 Flamethrowers ($100) & set Hold Position rally tiles, or train Engineers, Lv2 Medics ($85) & Lv3 Sappers ($90).

Abilities, Mini-Boss & Supply Drops

Spend Scrap on Overcharge ($200) & Napalm ($300)! Wave 6+ spawns a Brood Warden Mini-Boss every wave across all modes; Survival Wave 10s unleash the Hive Behemoth boss.

Engineer Upgrades (+20/+40/+70 HP/s) & Queues

Habitat ($110) queues 20 Civilians. Medics escort squads with green healing beams; Sappers plant up to 5 mines every 3s outside Safe Zones (click within 3 tiles to detonate); Engineers weld at $0!

7 Safe-Zone Towers (Mortar, Laser & Radar)

Sentry, Cryo, Sniper (30% HEADSHOT, Boss immune), Tesla (3/5/8), Mortar ($240) splash, Laser ($260) heavy beam (1.0x→3.0x), and Radar ($175) +15–25% aura target zombies only.

1. WALKER (walker)HP: 40 • Speed: 45 px/s • Dmg: 10 / 0.5s • Bounty: +$1 • Wave 1+

Standard infected shambler. Ignores civilians completely; targets Rescued Soldiers/Engineers within 300px, then green Safe Zone blocks. Routes around structures when an open lane exists.

2. RUNNER (runner)HP: 25 • Speed: 85 px/s • Dmg: 8 / 0.5s • Bounty: +$1 • Wave 2+

High-velocity sprinter appearing from Wave 2+. Only zombie type that chases and infects exposed civilians; also spawns immediately whenever an infected white-dot civilian completes incubation.

3. TANK (tank)HP: 220 • Speed: 25 px/s • Dmg: 32 / 0.5s • Bounty: +$10 • Wave 3+

Heavily armored juggernaut (Wave 3+). Soaks kinetic fire and pounds structures or Safe Zone blocks for 32 damage per strike. Counter with Cryo slow + Sniper railguns.

4. DEMOLISHER (demolisher)HP: 180 • Speed: 38 px/s • Dmg: 20 / 0.5s • Bounty: +$9 • Wave 6+

Base-breaching siege brute (crimson-black armor, hazard-orange ring & front spikes; unlocked Wave 6+, max 4 combined with Wreckers). Marches straight toward the nearest Safe Zone and smashes every structure in its path. Prioritized by Snipers!

5. WRECKER (wrecker)HP: 110 • Speed: 62 px/s • Dmg: 14 / 0.5s • Bounty: +$7 • Wave 6+

Anti-tower hunter (electric-violet chassis, hazard-yellow siege ring & claws; unlocked Wave 6+, max 4 combined with Demolishers). Actively hunts Turrets and Barracks first before advancing on Safe Zones. Prioritized by Snipers!

6. BROOD WARDEN (miniboss)HP: 450 • Speed: 30 px/s • Dmg: 24 / 0.5s • Bounty: +$45 • Wave 6+ (All Modes)

Heavily plated crimson-gold field Mini-Boss deploying every wave from Wave 6 onward across all modes. Immune to Sniper instant-kill headshots — focus sustained Laser beams or Gunner crossfire.

7. HIVE BEHEMOTH (behemoth)HP: 900 • Speed: 24 px/s • Dmg: 35 / 0.5s • Bounty: +$80 • Survival Wave 10, 20, 30...

Survival Mode apex brood boss spawning every 10th wave (Sniper headshot immune). Radiates a 120px crimson brood aura granting nearby zombies +20% movement speed and 25% damage reduction until eliminated.
