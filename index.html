TACTICAL OPERATIONS MANUAL (v2.6.0)

Single 3×3 Start & Line-Drag Barricades

Every map opens with a single 3×3 Safe Zone (+9% DMG). Expand Safe Zones ($10, Key 7) toward breaches; all other buildings & towers build only on Safe Zones. Barricades ($10, Key 1) build up to 3 tiles out via line-drag.

Perimeter Commandos & Breach Destruction

Commandos (150/210/280 HP, 24/34/46 dmg, 120/140/160px) actively intercept zombies/breaches within ≤3 tiles of Safe Zones, gain +15% RoF on Safe Zones, kite backward at <120px, and are the ONLY unit able to destroy Spawn Breaches (1.25× dmg)!

Bidirectional 10-Wave Scaling & Supply Drops

Every 10 waves awards a Milestone Supply Drop (+$150+) and scales hordes up (diminishing sqrt) or down to 0.75x if battered! Wave 20 unlocks 2/2 Habitats; ≤5 Safe Zones grants +25% Kill Scrap.

Engineer Upgrades (+20/+40/+70 HP/s) & Queues

Habitat ($110) queues up to 20 Civilians. Upgrading Engineering scales linked Engineers to +20 / +40 / +70 HP/s ($0 welding across Safe Zones & Barricades). Randomized zombie paths fan across perimeter lanes.

Zombie-Only Towers & 40% Sniper HEADSHOT

Sentries, Cryo (slows movement + attack speed) & Snipers (40% HEADSHOT) target zombies exclusively — expand $10 Safe Zones within 3 tiles of a Spawn Breach so Commandos can assault it!

Visual Spotlight Tutorial & Controls

Click ★ Tutorial on startup for visual arrow-guided training! Q Select/Clear • W Upgrade • S Salvage (2x Lv2/3) • 1–0 Build • E Release • ↻ Reset / Menu.

☠ ZOMBIE BESTIARY & THREAT INTEL (v2.6.0)

25% Random Brute-Breach Trait: Any spawned Walker, Runner, Tank, or Wrecker has a 25% chance (100% on Demolisher) to smash straight toward Safe Zones, dealing a fixed unscaled 12 damage (16 damage for Demolishers) per strike against structures & Safe Zones.

Standard infected shambler. Pursues nearest uninfected civilian, then Rescued Soldiers/Engineers within 300px, then green Safe Zone blocks. Routes around structures when an open lane exists.

2. RUNNER (runner)HP: 25 • Speed: 85 px/s • Dmg: 8 / 0.5s • Bounty: +$8 • Wave 2+

High-velocity sprinter appearing from Wave 2+. Closes distance rapidly to bite exposed civilians; also spawns immediately whenever an infected white-dot civilian completes incubation.

3. TANK (tank)HP: 220 • Speed: 25 px/s • Dmg: 32 / 0.5s • Bounty: +$20 • Wave 3+

Heavily armored juggernaut (Wave 3+). Soaks kinetic fire and pounds structures or Safe Zone blocks for 32 damage per strike. Counter with Cryo slow + Sniper railguns.

Base-breaching siege brute (crimson-black armor, hazard-orange ring & front spikes; unlocked Wave 6+, max 4 combined with Wreckers). Marches straight toward the nearest Safe Zone and smashes every structure in its path. Prioritized by Snipers!

Anti-tower hunter (electric-violet chassis, hazard-yellow siege ring & claws; unlocked Wave 6+, max 4 combined with Demolishers). Actively hunts Turrets, Bunkers, and Barracks first before advancing on Safe Zones. Prioritized by Snipers!
